Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

EduSpark (eduspark.de5.net) is a free study app for students preparing for CBSE and BSER Class 10/12 and competitive exams such as SSC, BSTC and REET. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Profile. Your name, username, profile photo and the board and class you choose. This is saved on your device, and also in our Firebase (Firestore) database so you can get it back when you sign in on another device.
  • Google Sign-In. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account name, email address and user ID through Firebase Authentication.
  • Device information. A device ID and the time you were last active.
  • Notifications. A push notification token, only if you allow notifications.
  • Doubts. When you submit a doubt: your name (or "Anonymous"), the question, subject, topic and board.
  • Consent record. The policy version you accepted and the date (from the checkbox on the login page). This stays on your device.
  • Study progress and settings. Mostly stored on your device.

We do not ask for your phone number, home address or payment details.

2. How we use it

To run the app: show study content, answer doubts, remember your profile and progress, and send notifications. We also use it to improve the app and fix errors. We process your data on the basis of your consent.

3. Crash reports and analytics

Crash reports start when you tick the Terms/Privacy checkbox on the login page (the checkbox says so). Usage analytics and recordings are currently switched off; before we ever turn them on we will ask for a separate permission, and only for users aged 18 or above. To withdraw your consent, log out from the Profile page; the checkbox will be shown again at your next login.

  • Crash reports (Sentry). A technical record of errors, such as browser or phone type and the error message. Your name and email are not sent.
  • Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics) and screen-interaction recordings and heatmaps (Microsoft Clarity). These show which parts of the app are used most. Text you type is masked in recordings. They are currently off. If we enable them in future, it will be only with a separate permission and only for users aged 18 or above.

4. Services we use

Each service below has its own privacy policy.

  • Google Firebase for Authentication, the Firestore database and push notifications (Cloud Messaging).
  • Cloudflare to run the app's background server, for example for AI answers.
  • AI doubt solver. When you tap the AI answer button, your doubt (with its subject, topic and board) is sent through Cloudflare to a Google AI model that writes the answer. AI answers can be wrong, so please check important things with a teacher or textbook. Do not put personal details such as a phone number or address in a doubt.
  • ImageKit to load images used in quizzes.
  • UptimeRobot to check that the site is online. It does not receive information about individual users.
  • Sentry (crash reports, after you tick the checkbox). Firebase Analytics and Microsoft Clarity are currently off, as described above.
  • Google AdSense, as described below.

5. Advertising

EduSpark includes Google AdSense, and ads may be shown in the app. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies or device identifiers to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to EduSpark and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or visit aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising. To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We do not intend to show personalised ads to children.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed above so the app can work, when the law requires it, or to protect the security of EduSpark and its users.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can view and change your profile on the Profile page.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time from Profile → "Delete account and data". This removes your profile, username, photo, notification token, doubts and login account.
  • If you cannot open the app, follow the steps on the Account Delete page or email us.
  • You can withdraw your consent by logging out from the Profile page.

8. How long we keep data

As long as your account exists or until you delete it. After deletion, the data listed above is removed. Crash data, if you ticked the checkbox, stay with Google, Microsoft and Sentry under their own rules, and we do not link them to your name.

9. Students and children

EduSpark is made for students. Users under 18 should use the app and Google Sign-In with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. We keep usage analytics and screen recordings off for users under 18, and we do not knowingly collect more personal information than the app needs.

10. Security

Data is stored in Google Firebase with access rules and is sent over HTTPS. Firebase servers may be located outside India. No online system is completely secure, so please keep your Google account safe.

11. Content and copyright

EduSpark is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with or approved by CBSE, BSER, SSC or any other examination body. If you believe any content in EduSpark infringes your copyright, email us with the details and the location of the content, and we will review it and remove it promptly where appropriate.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change. For larger changes the app may ask for your consent again.
