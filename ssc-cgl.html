SSC CGL Preparation Guide

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The Combined Graduate Level (CGL) examination, conducted by the Staff Selection Commission, is one of the most popular graduate-level government exams in India. This guide explains how to organise preparation around the main sections.

Main sections in the first tier

SectionWhat it testsHow to practise
General Intelligence & ReasoningAnalogies, series, coding-decoding, syllogisms, puzzles, non-verbal reasoningDaily timed sets; learn shortcuts only after you understand the logic
General AwarenessStatic GK, history, polity, geography, science, current affairsShort notes, weekly revision, monthly current-affairs recap
Quantitative AptitudeArithmetic, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, data interpretationFormula sheet, chapter-wise practice, speed drills
English ComprehensionGrammar, vocabulary, error spotting, cloze test, reading comprehensionDaily reading, vocabulary list, grammar rules with examples

The exam has more than one stage and the pattern is updated from time to time, so read the latest official notification for tiers, marking and negative marking.

A 90-day outline

  1. Days 1–30: foundation. Cover every topic once. Make a formula sheet for Maths and a vocabulary list for English.
  2. Days 31–60: practice. Chapter-wise questions, then sectional timed tests. Maintain an error log.
  3. Days 61–90: tests and revision. Full mock tests, analysis of each mock, and revision of the error log and notes.

How to use previous year questions

Solve earlier shifts and years topic-wise first, then as full papers. Note which topics repeat, which type of question takes you the most time, and where you lose marks to silly mistakes. Previous year questions show the difficulty level and style, but they are not a substitute for learning the concepts.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Taking many mocks but never analysing them.
  • Ignoring negative marking and guessing blindly.
  • Leaving English for the end – it improves slowly and needs daily exposure.
