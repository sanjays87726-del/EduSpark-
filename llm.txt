# EduSpark

> EduSpark is a student-focused learning platform designed to help Indian students study, practise, and prepare for board and competitive examinations.

- [Privacy Policy](https://eduspark.de5.net/privacy.html): Privacy information
- [Terms](https://eduspark.de5.net/terms.html): Website terms

## Learning Resources

EduSpark's intended audience includes students preparing for Class 10 and Class 12 board examinations, as well as learners preparing for SSC, REET, BSTC and other competitive examinations. Depending on the content available on the website, students may find study resources, quizzes, previous-year-question practice, and doubt-support features.
