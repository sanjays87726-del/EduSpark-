BSER (RBSE) Class 10 Study Guide

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The Board of Secondary Education, Rajasthan (BSER, also written RBSE) conducts the Secondary (Class 10) examination for lakhs of students every year. This guide shows a simple, repeatable method to prepare, in either Hindi or English medium.

What to study

Core subjects are generally Hindi, English, Mathematics, Science and Social Science, with a third language or optional subject depending on your school. Download the latest syllabus from the board and prepare strictly from it, because topics can be added or removed between years.

A step-by-step plan

  1. Textbook first. Read the prescribed textbook line by line. Questions in board papers are closely tied to the textbook language, so underline definitions and key terms.
  2. Write, do not just read. Practise writing answers within the expected length. For Social Science, learn to organise a long answer in a heading, three or four points and a conclusion.
  3. Previous year papers. Solve the last several years of papers. You will notice that important topics repeat, and you will learn how the paper is structured.
  4. Weak-topic list. After each paper, list the topics where you lost marks and give them extra time the next week.
  5. Final month. Revise from your short notes, redo the error list and take two or three full papers in exam time.

Subject tips

  • Mathematics: daily practice of every exercise type; write formulae before solving; check units and signs.
  • Science: neat labelled diagrams, balanced equations and numericals with units.
  • Social Science: maps, timelines and short keyword-rich answers; practise Rajasthan-specific topics where they appear in the syllabus.
  • Hindi and English: grammar, letter and essay formats, and clean handwriting.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Studying from guess papers instead of the syllabus.
  • Leaving one subject completely for the last week.
  • Not practising with a timer, then running out of time in the exam hall.
