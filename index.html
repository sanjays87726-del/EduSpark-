🔒 Privacy Policy

EduSpark ek free study app hai (CBSE, BSER aur competition exams ke students ke liye). Ye policy batati hai ki hum kaunsa data collect karte hain aur uska use kaise karte hain.

1. Hum kya collect karte hain

• Aapka naam aur chuna hua Board/Class (sirf aapke device par save hota hai)
• Agar aap Google से Sign In karte hain, to Google account ka naam aur email Firebase Authentication ke through humein milta hai
• Agar aap Doubt submit karte hain, to aapka naam (ya "Anonymous"), sawaal, subject, aur board Firestore database mein save hota hai

2. Data ka use kaise hota hai

Ye data sirf app ki study features (content dikhana, doubts ka jawab dena, progress yaad rakhna) ke liye use hota hai। Hum aapka data kisi third party ko bechte ya share nahi karte।

3. Third-party services

Ye app Google Firebase (Authentication aur Firestore Database) use karta hai data store karne ke liye। Google ki apni privacy policy alag se apply hoti hai।

4. Ads

Is app mein koi third-party advertisement nahi dikhaya jaata।

5. Data delete karwana

Agar aap apna data delete karwana chahte hain (jaise submit kiya hua doubt ya profile), to neeche diye contact par sampark karein।

6. Bachchon ki privacy

Ye app students ke liye bana hai। Hum jaan-boojh kar kisi bhi bachche se unnecessary personal information collect nahi karte। Agar koi minor Google Sign-In use karta hai, ye unke parent/guardian ki janकari mein hona chahiye।

7. Sampark करें

Kisi bhi sawaal ya data delete request ke liye humse Instagram par sampark karein: @bhupesh_jatav_02
