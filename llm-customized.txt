# EduSpark

> EduSpark is a student-focused learning platform for Indian learners preparing for school board and competitive examinations.

- [Home](https://eduspark.live/): Main EduSpark website
- [About](https://eduspark.live/about.html): About the platform
- [Contact](https://eduspark.live/contact.html): Contact and feedback
- [Privacy Policy](https://eduspark.live/privacy.html): Privacy information
- [Terms](https://eduspark.live/terms.html): Website terms

## Learners and Study Areas

EduSpark is intended for students preparing for:
- CBSE Class 10 and Class 12
- BSER/RBSE Class 10 and Class 12
- SSC examinations, including SSC-CHSL and SSC-CGL
- REET and BSTC/D.El.Ed

## Learning Features and Content

Depending on what is currently published on the website, EduSpark may provide:
- Study notes and learning resources
- Practice quizzes and tests
- Previous-year-question practice (PYQ)
- Subject and topic-based practice
- Doubt-support features
