About EduSpark

EduSpark is a free learning platform built for CBSE and BSER (RBSE) board students in Classes 10 and 12. It exists on one simple idea: quality exam preparation shouldn't depend on how much a family can spend on coaching or study material.

What you'll find here

  • Previous year question papers (PYQs), organised by subject and year
  • Subject-wise notes and PDFs
  • Practice quizzes with detailed answer keys
  • Updates on live classes
  • A private space to ask doubts
  • A personal profile to keep track of your progress

How it works

EduSpark is built as a lightweight, installable web app, so it runs on almost any phone straight from the browser — no app-store download required — and saved content keeps working even when you're offline.

Who's behind it

EduSpark is built and maintained independently, with new content and features added regularly based on direct student feedback.
