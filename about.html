About EduSpark

EduSpark is a free learning platform for CBSE and BSER (RBSE) Class 10 and 12 students, and for learners preparing for SSC, REET, BSTC and similar exams. It exists on one simple idea: good exam preparation should not depend on how much a family can spend on coaching or study material.

What you will find

  • Practice quizzes with answers and explanations, in practice mode and timed mock-test mode.
  • Previous year question papers (PYQs) organised by board, subject and year.
  • Study material and notes by class and subject.
  • A doubts section where you can ask questions and, if you wish, get a quick AI-assisted explanation.
  • Study guides that explain how to prepare: CBSE 10, CBSE 12, BSER 10, BSER 12, SSC CGL, SSC CHSL, REET & BSTC and study tips.

Who is behind EduSpark

EduSpark is designed, built and maintained by Bhupesh, a student developer from Jaipur, Rajasthan, who also answers support messages. It is an independent project and is not run by any board, coaching institute or government body.

How we create and check content

  • Quizzes and explanations are prepared from textbooks, official syllabi and previous year papers, then checked before they are published. The admin panel rejects malformed quiz files so that broken questions do not reach students.
  • AI is used only for the optional doubt-solving feature. AI answers are labelled and can be wrong; we ask students to verify them.
  • If you find a mistake, tell us and we will correct or remove it. See Contact.

Our principles

  • Free to learn: core study features stay free for students.
  • Honest about limits: we say clearly when content is unofficial, when answers come from AI and when information may be out of date.
  • Privacy first: we ask for as little data as possible, keep analytics off for minors and let you delete everything.
  • Listen to students: most new features and corrections come directly from messages sent to us.

How the app works

EduSpark is an installable web app: it runs in your browser, can be added to your home screen, needs no app-store download, and keeps content you have already opened available when your internet is slow or offline.

Your privacy

We collect only what the app needs, we do not sell your data, and you can delete your account at any time. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
